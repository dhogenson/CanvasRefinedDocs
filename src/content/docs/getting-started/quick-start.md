Already [installed Canvas Refined](/getting-started/install/)? Use this guide for your first setup.

## 1. Open the settings

Open your browser's extensions menu and select **Canvas Refined**. This opens the extension popup, not Canvas's own settings. Pin the extension to your toolbar for easier access.

Use **Search features...** at the top of the popup if you cannot find an option. The documentation site's search searches these guides; it does not search your Canvas courses.

![Setup diagram: open the browser extensions menu, select Canvas Refined, then search for a feature or open its settings section.](/guides/first-setup.svg)

*Setup diagram, not a screenshot. The popup's layout and labels can vary by version.*

## 2. Check your dashboard

Open your school's Canvas dashboard and refresh it if it was already open. On a new installation, expect:

- Dark mode.
- Assignment lists and grades on course cards, where data is available.
- The Better Todo List.
- A GPA calculator and Grade Analytics.

If dashboard widgets are missing, try Canvas's **Card View**. If nothing changes at all, follow the [installation checks](/getting-started/install/#not-seeing-any-changes).

## 3. Make it yours

1. Open **Edit Dark Mode** (or **Dark Mode**) and choose a preset.
2. Find **Better Todo List**, then choose a **Time frame** and **Max items to show** that suit your workload.
3. Open **Widgets**, find **GPA calculator**, and review its grade scale settings. Older versions have a separate **GPA Settings** button. Match the scale and course credits to your school before relying on the estimate.

Change one setting at a time so you can see what it does. Refresh Canvas if a change does not appear immediately.

## Some things to explore next

See the feature pages for [dark mode](/features/dark-mode/), [dashboard cards](/features/dashboard-cards/), [the to-do list](/features/better-todo-list/), and [grades and GPA](/features/grades-and-gpa/).

You can also enable [Global Canvas Search](/features/global-canvas-search/), [Dashboard Notes](/features/dashboard-notes/), or [Quiz Safe Mode](/features/quiz-safe-mode/). These are off by default.

For fonts, backgrounds, widget placement, and saving your setup, see [Customization](/customization/).

:::tip[Know how to undo a change]
Turn an individual feature off in the popup, or temporarily disable the extension in your browser's extension manager. You do not need to uninstall it to test Canvas without it.
:::
