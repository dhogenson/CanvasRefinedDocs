This page should get you up to date with the basics of Canvas Refined and how to use it.

If you have not already installed Canvas Refined go to the [install page](/getting-started/install) for more details.

## Configure Canvas Refined

To configure Canvas Refined, go to your browser's extension menu, and click the Canvas Refined icon. Note that you can't change Canvas Refined's settings though Canvas itself. If you want to, pin the extension for easier access.

## What's already enabled

On a fresh install, Canvas Refined enables
- Dark mode
- Better todo list
- Card assignments and card grades
- GPA calculator
- Grade Analytics

You can view any of these settings from the extension, feel free to change any of it.

## Pick your appearance

Open **Edit Dark Mode** in the extension popup to choose a preset or adjust colors. Enable **Automatic dark mode** to follow a schedule or your device's settings, or turn off **Dark mode** for Canvas's normal appearance.

For more customization, open **Styles** to change fonts and backgrounds, or **Card Customization** to adjust dashboard cards. Some card changes may need a refresh.

## Set up your todo list

The **Better Todo List** is enabled by default and appears on your Canvas dashboard. To customize it, open the extension popup and find **Better Todo List**.

Adjust **Time frame** to choose how far ahead to look and **Max items to show** to control the list's length. You can also turn on assignment scores or turn off completion confetti.

## Check your GPA scale

Open **GPA Settings** in the extension popup and check that the grade cutoffs match your school's scale. Choose a **+/−** or **By Letter** preset, or adjust the values yourself. Treat Canvas Refined's calculated GPA as an estimate, and not your official GPA.

## Optional features to try

These are off by default. Enable them in the extension popup if you'd like:

- **Global Canvas Search:** Press **Ctrl+K** (or **Cmd+K** on Mac) to search modules and assignments across courses.
- **Dashboard Notes:** Keep notes on your dashboard, with Markdown support.
- **Quiz Safe Mode:** Turn off most Canvas Refined features on quiz pages for a more default Canvas experience.

## Not seeing changes?

Refresh Canvas and check that the extension is enabled and allowed to access your school's site. If the site isn't detected, click **+ Add canvas** in the popup and enter your school's Canvas URL.
