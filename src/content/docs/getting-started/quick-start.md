Already [installed Canvas Refined](/getting-started/install/)? Here's how to get started.

## Open the settings

Open your browser's extension menu and click **Canvas Refined**. These settings are separate from Canvas's own settings. Pin the extension if you want easier access.

## Check your dashboard

Open your school's Canvas dashboard and refresh it if it was already open. Dark mode, the Better Todo List, card assignments and grades, the GPA calculator, and Grade Analytics are enabled by default.

## Make it yours

1. Open **Edit Dark Mode** in the popup and choose a preset you like.
2. Find **Better Todo List** and choose your **Time frame** and **Max items to show**.
3. Open **GPA Settings** and check that the grading scale matches your school's. The calculated GPA is an estimate, not your official GPA.

Use **Search features…** in the popup if you can't find a setting.

## Explore next

See the feature guides for [dark mode](/features/dark-mode/), [dashboard cards](/features/dashboard-cards/), [the todo list](/features/better-todo-list/), and [grades and GPA](/features/grades-and-gpa/).

You can also try [Global Canvas Search](/features/global-canvas-search/), [Dashboard Notes](/features/dashboard-notes/), or [Quiz Safe Mode](/features/quiz-safe-mode/). These are off by default.

If nothing changes, see the [installation checks](/getting-started/install/#not-seeing-any-changes).
