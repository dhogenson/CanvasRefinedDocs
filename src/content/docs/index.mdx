Canvas Refined is a browser extension for Canvas LMS. These guides help you get started, find the settings you need, and understand what each feature can do and can't do.

## What would you like to do?

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Make Canvas your own" icon="setting">
    Choose [dark mode colors](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/dark-mode/), adjust your
    [course cards](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/dashboard-cards/), or explore
    [fonts, backgrounds, and widgets](/CanvasRefinedDocs/customization/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stay organized" icon="pencil">
    Review coursework with the [Better Todo List](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/better-todo-list/)
    and keep personal reminders in [Dashboard Notes](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/dashboard-notes/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Understand your grades" icon="document">
    Set your grade scale, credits, and course weights with the
    [Grades and GPA guide](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/grades-and-gpa/). Know what the estimate includes before relying on it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Find course content" icon="open-book">
    Search modules and assignments across courses with
    [Global Canvas Search](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/global-canvas-search/).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Already installed?

- **Something is not working?** Start with [Troubleshooting](/CanvasRefinedDocs/help/troubleshooting/).
- **Preparing for a quiz?** Read [Quiz Safe Mode](/CanvasRefinedDocs/features/quiz-safe-mode/) and follow your school's exam requirements.
- **Want to know where your information goes?** Read [Privacy and Permissions](/CanvasRefinedDocs/privacy/).
- **Need help or have an idea?** Visit [Support and Feedback](/CanvasRefinedDocs/help/support/).

New to the extension? The [Overview](/CanvasRefinedDocs/getting-started/overview/) explains the defaults and helps you choose where to begin.
