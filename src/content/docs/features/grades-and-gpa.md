---
title: Grades and GPA
description: Check your GPA scale and enable grade analysis tools.
---

The GPA calculator estimates your GPA from your Canvas grades. **Grade Analytics** adds tools for analyzing grades. Both are enabled by default.

## Use it

Find the GPA calculator on your dashboard. Open **GPA Settings** in the extension popup and check that the grade cutoffs match your school's scale. Choose a **+/−** or **By Letter** preset, or adjust the values yourself.

## Settings

Under **GPA calculator**, choose whether to show weighted or cumulative GPA, or move the calculator to the top. Under **Grade Analytics**, you can enable **Colored grade zones**.

Treat the calculated GPA as an estimate, not your official GPA. Its usefulness depends on the available Canvas grades and your grading settings.
