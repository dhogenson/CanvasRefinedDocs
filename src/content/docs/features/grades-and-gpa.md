The GPA calculator estimates GPA from available Canvas grades. **Grade Analytics** adds grade analysis tools. Both are **enabled by default**.

:::caution[An estimate, not your official GPA]
Your school's rules may differ from the calculator's assumptions. Check course inclusion, credits, grading cutoffs, and weighting. Use your school's academic record for official GPA.
:::

## Set up the GPA calculator

1. Open your Canvas dashboard in **Card View** and locate the GPA widget.
2. In current versions, select **Edit Calculator** on the widget to review course percentages, credits, and weights.
3. Open **Widgets** in the extension popup and find **GPA calculator** for display and grade-scale options. Older versions use a separate **GPA Settings** button.
4. Choose a **+/−** or **By Letter** scale preset, or edit the percentage cutoffs and GPA values to match your school.
5. Review each included course. Set its credits and weight rather than assuming the defaults match your transcript.

## Understand the results

- **Current:** A credit-weighted average of the included courses' grade-point values.
- **Weighted:** Adds course-type bonuses before averaging. The reviewed source adds 0.5 points for Honors and 1 point for AP; those values may not match your school's policy.
- **Cumulative:** Combines current courses with the prior GPA and prior credits you supply. It does not automatically import your complete transcript. The weighted display setting also affects which current-course points are used.

A course marked not to count is excluded. Hidden or date-restricted courses may not appear. Missing or invalid grades and credits can prevent a course from contributing; do not assume a blank value is counted as a failing grade.

Changing a percentage in the calculator is a what-if estimate. It does not change your official Canvas grade.

## Display settings

Under **Widgets → GPA calculator**, choose whether to show weighted or cumulative GPA and adjust the widget's position. Older versions may instead offer a setting to move it to the top.

## Grade Analytics

Find **Grade Analytics** in the popup to enable or disable the grade tools. **Colored grade zones** is an optional display setting.

These tools depend on the grade information Canvas returns. They cannot reveal unreleased scores or override instructor grading rules.

## If the estimate looks wrong

Compare the calculator's included courses, percentages, credits, scale, and weights with your school's records. A matching percentage alone does not guarantee a matching GPA.

For missing grades, see [An assignment or grade is missing](/CanvasRefinedDocs/help/troubleshooting/#an-assignment-or-grade-is-missing).
