title: Dashboard Cards

**Card assignments** and **Card Grades** show coursework and grades on your dashboard's course cards. Both are enabled by default.

## Use it

Open your Canvas dashboard to see your course cards. In the extension popup, use **Card assignments** and **Card Grades** to turn these features on or off.

## Settings

- **Card assignments:** Adjust the item limit, due date format, and whether to show past-due or hide completed items.
- **Card Grades:** Choose letter grades or show grades only on hover.
- **Card Customization:** Change card colors, images, spacing, and size.

Some card customization changes require refreshing Canvas. Available grade information depends on what your courses provide.
