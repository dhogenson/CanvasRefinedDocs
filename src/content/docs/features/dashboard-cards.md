title: Dashboard Cards
description: Show assignments and grades on course cards and customize their appearance.
---

**Card assignments** adds coursework to your dashboard's course cards. **Card Grades** adds available course grades. Both are **enabled by default**.

## Use it

1. Open your Canvas dashboard in **Card View**.
2. Look for assignments and grades on your course cards.
3. Open the extension popup to turn **Card assignments** or **Card Grades** on or off.

## Assignment settings

- **Max items to show:** Limit how many assignments appear on each card.
- **Show past due:** Include overdue work.
- **Hide completed:** Hide items marked complete.
- **Use dd/mm** and **Relative due dates:** Adjust date presentation.
- **Equal height:** Keep assignment areas more consistent across cards.

The item limit and filters can hide work. Open the course's own assignment page when you need the full list.

## Grade settings

Use **Show letter grade** to change grade presentation, or **Show only when hovering** to reduce how often grades are visible.

Grades depend on what Canvas makes available for each course. A missing card grade does not necessarily mean the course has no graded work.

## Customize card appearance

Open **Card Settings** (called **Card Customization** in some versions) to adjust course card details and appearance. Options include colors, images, spacing, size, and additional card styles.

Change a small number of settings at a time, then refresh Canvas if necessary. See [Customization](/CanvasRefinedDocs/customization/) for backgrounds and other dashboard settings.

## Limitations and common problems

These features are designed around dashboard course cards, not every Canvas dashboard view. Hidden courses, unavailable grades, filters, and item limits affect what you see.

If a card or grade is missing, compare it with the original Canvas course and follow [An assignment or grade is missing](/CanvasRefinedDocs/help/troubleshooting/#an-assignment-or-grade-is-missing).
