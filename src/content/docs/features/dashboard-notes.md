---
title: Dashboard Notes
description: Keep notes on your Canvas dashboard.
---

Dashboard Notes gives you a place for notes on your dashboard, with Markdown support. It's off by default.

## Use it

Enable **Dashboard Notes** in the extension popup, then open your Canvas dashboard to write your notes. You can use Markdown such as **bold text** and lists to format them.

Turn the feature off in the popup to hide the notes area. Notes are for your own reference, not assignment submissions.
