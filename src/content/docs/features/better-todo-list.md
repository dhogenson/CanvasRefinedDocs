description: View upcoming coursework and choose how your dashboard todo list looks.
---

The Better Todo List organizes coursework on your Canvas dashboard. It's enabled by default.

## Use it

Open your dashboard to find the list. To adjust it or turn it off, find **Better Todo List** in the extension popup.

## Settings

- **Time frame:** Choose how far ahead to look.
- **Max items to show:** Control the list's length.
- Show assignment scores, preview items on hover, or turn off completion confetti.
- Open the additional options for settings such as 24-hour time and hiding recent feedback.

The time frame and item limit affect what appears, so the list may not show every assignment.
